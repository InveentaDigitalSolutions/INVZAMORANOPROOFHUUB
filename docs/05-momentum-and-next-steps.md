## The five-year trajectory

Over the next five years the Tropical Proof Hub strategy is expected to:

- **increase adoption** of validated production practices;
- **protect nutritional quality and food safety** under climate stress;
- **improve resource-use efficiency** per unit of output;
- **establish full MRV/QA traceability** supported by independent verification.

Within this framework, **nutritional yield** — protein quality and micronutrient delivery per hectare and per unit of energy — becomes a central measure of agricultural performance.

## The board decision

The decision is not whether Zamorano should become a research university, but whether it should formally embrace the role for which it is uniquely positioned: the place where scientific discoveries developed around the world are validated, integrated, and transformed into reliable agricultural solutions for the tropics. Approving the Tropical Proof Hub — and the partner-based model and MRV/QA governance that operationalize it — is an affirmation of Zamorano's founding *learning-by-doing* mission, not a departure from it.
